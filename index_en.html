A premier equestrian centre

Based in Overijse, minutes from Brussels and on the edge of the Sonian Forest, Royal Country Riding Club sits in a green, privileged setting, ideal for living your passion for horses to the full.

The Country has two outdoor arenas, a covered school, 60 stables, a walker, extensive paddocks and pasture, and direct access to the Sonian Forest.

Whether you ride for pleasure or at a high level, on your own, with family or friends, the Country is a place where love of riding, a welcoming community and horse welfare come together. We aim to give everyone a quality environment that supports riding and the joy of sharing that passion day after day.

A club that keeps evolving, a passion that brings us together

Today the club offers excellent facilities, recently upgraded to welcome horses and riders in the best possible conditions:

  • A fully renovated covered arena, 22 × 45 metres
  • Two outdoor arenas, 65 × 75 metres and 25 × 45 metres
  • 60 comfortable stables, including a section that has been completely rebuilt
  • A new walker to support movement and horse welfare
  • Winter paddocks so horses can still enjoy turnout during the cold season
  • Plenty of pasture for horses to relax outdoors
  • Relaxation areas and equipment suited to everyday life at the club
  • Direct access to the Sonian Forest, with dozens of kilometres of bridle paths for horses and riders

Livery & services

At Royal Country Club we look after your horse day to day and make life easier for you. Our priorities are your horse’s welfare and your enjoyment of riding at the club.

  • Full livery

    Stable, hay and tailored feed.

  • Horse training

    Lunge work and ridden sessions.

  • Clipping

    According to season and need.

  • Health care

    Vaccinations and worming with the club vet.

  • Competitions

    Transport and logistics organised with our instructors.

  • Turnout & exercise

    Pasture and/or walker.

  • Private lessons

    Show jumping, dressage and general riding with our instructors, or with your own coach.

Riding school

Give your children a distinctive riding experience that blends passion, teaching and a friendly atmosphere. In a beautiful setting and with top-quality facilities, our school is designed to guide young riders from their very first contact with horses through to more advanced work.

  • A qualified, dedicated team

    Our certified instructors are motivated and supportive, putting their expertise at the service of each pupil’s development.

  • Small groups (3 to 4 riders max)

    With close individual attention, every child progresses at their own pace, safely and much faster.

  • A well-rounded approach to horses

    Learning to ride starts in the stable yard: we teach riding basics as well as care, grooming and getting the horse ready before and after each session.

  • A warm, family-friendly atmosphere

    Enjoyment in the saddle and respect for the animal are at the heart of every lesson, in a spirit of sharing and camaraderie.
